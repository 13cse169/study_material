Neural Networks — Introduction

Perceptron

Single neuron: weighted inputs → activation. The building block of everything.

Multilayer Perceptron (MLP)

Fully connected layers, activation functions (ReLU, sigmoid, tanh). Forward pass.

Backpropagation

Chain rule applied end-to-end. Compute gradients, update weights with optimizer.

Optimizers

SGD, Adam, AdaGrad, RMSprop — how learning rate adapts during training.

Regularization

Dropout, L1/L2 weight decay, batch normalization — prevent overfitting in deep nets.
